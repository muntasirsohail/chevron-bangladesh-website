01 / ABOUT

Energy for a growing Bangladesh.

Chevron operates three natural-gas fields in northeastern Bangladesh: Bibiyana, Jalalabad and Moulavi Bazar. The company's Bangladesh operations focus on safe, reliable production, operational excellence, technology and long-term partnerships.
